## Product Information

### Core Product

**Product Name:** School Picker

**One-Line Description:**
An AI-powered tool that helps Sydney parents find and compare private schools that genuinely fit their child's needs.

**Detailed Description:**
School Picker helps parents navigate the overwhelming private school landscape in Sydney. With hundreds of schools each offering different education styles, academic tracks, cultures, price points, gender compositions, and extracurricular strengths, the current process is fragmented and stressful. Parents rely on Facebook groups, word of mouth, and expensive consultants.

School Picker cuts through the noise with AI-powered recommendations and side-by-side school comparisons. Parents input what matters most to them (location, budget, values, child's interests) and get a shortlist of schools that actually fit, plus the context to make a confident decision.

The business model is freemium: basic search and recommendations are free; deeper comparison tools, school fit scores, and personalized guidance are premium features.

### Product Categories

**Primary Category:** EdTech / School Discovery

**Secondary Categories:** AI Recommendations, Comparison Tools, Parenting

**Key Features:**
1. AI-powered school recommendations based on parent priorities
2. Side-by-side school comparison (academics, culture, cost, proximity, extracurriculars)
3. School profiles with education style, gender composition, fees, and student life
4. Proximity filter and map view
5. Saved shortlists and shareable comparisons

## Value Proposition

### Problem Statement

**The Problem:**
Sydney parents choosing private schools face hundreds of options with no good way to compare them. Schools differ on education philosophy, academic performance, culture, cost ($5k-$40k+/year), gender composition, location, and extracurricular specialisations (sports, music, drama, STEM). The information is scattered across school websites, Facebook groups, and paid consultants. The decision is high-stakes and irreversible enough that parents spend months researching with no structured help.

### Solution Statement

**Our Solution:**
School Picker gives parents a single place to discover, filter, and compare Sydney private schools. AI-powered recommendations ask parents what matters most (location, values, child's interests, budget) and surface the best-fit schools. Side-by-side comparison tools let parents evaluate shortlisted schools on the criteria that matter to them. The result: a confident, well-informed decision without paying a consultant.

### Unique Value Proposition

**What makes us different:**
1. AI recommendations tailored to each family's specific priorities (not just a directory)
2. Structured comparison across dimensions that actually matter to parents (not just league tables)
3. Free starting point that removes the cost barrier of traditional school consulting

**Why customers choose us over alternatives:**
- vs. Facebook groups: Structured data and AI recommendations vs. anecdotal opinions and noise
- vs. School consultants: Free (or much cheaper), available 24/7, no conflict of interest
- vs. Niche/GreatSchools: Sydney-specific, richer data, personalised to the family not just rankings
- vs. School websites: Neutral comparison across schools vs. each school's marketing pitch

### Competitive Landscape

**Direct Competitors:**

**Competitor 1: School consultants (e.g., private advisors)**
- Positioning: Expert, personalised guidance for families
- Strengths: Deep local knowledge, trusted relationships, handles application process
- Weaknesses: Expensive ($200-500/hr), not scalable, potential conflicts of interest, not available 24/7
- Pricing: $500-$3,000+ per engagement

**Competitor 2: Facebook parent groups**
- Positioning: Peer advice and community knowledge
- Strengths: Free, real parent experiences, local and current
- Weaknesses: Unstructured, noisy, biased, no comparison capability, overwhelming
- Pricing: Free

**Competitor 3: Individual school websites**
- Positioning: Official school information
- Strengths: Authoritative, detailed, up-to-date on that school
- Weaknesses: Marketing-heavy, no cross-school comparison, 50+ tabs open to compare
- Pricing: Free

**Indirect Competitors:**
- Word of mouth / personal networks - trusted but limited to who you know
- Generic review sites (not Australia-focused, not private school specific)

**Our Positioning:**
The structured, AI-powered alternative to consulting a friend or hiring an expensive advisor. Fast, free to start, and built for Sydney families who want to make a confident decision without the noise.
