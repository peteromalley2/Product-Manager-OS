# Decision Log

> Major decisions are logged here so agents don't relitigate them and the founder has a clear record.
> Format: `[Date] — [Decision] — [Rationale] — [Who decided]`

---

## How to Use

Agents append entries when significant decisions are made during a run.
Founders can add entries manually for decisions made outside the system.

A "significant decision" is anything that:
- Changes the product direction
- Changes the tech stack
- Changes the target customer or pricing
- Resolves a trade-off that took real thought
- Would be confusing to future-you without context
